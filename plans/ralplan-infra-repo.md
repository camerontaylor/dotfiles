## RALPLAN-DR

**Principles**
1. **Contracts are load-bearing.** C1/C3/C4/C5/C6(G1)/C7 implemented verbatim; any step that cannot is marked **ESCALATE**. One exists: C3's offline-convergence-on-return clause under the chosen Phase A transport — escalated-or-owner-waived at A7 (D3 treatment).
